Both routes are graded the same way: on your interpretation of the numbers and on the decisions you recommend. Choosing Route 2 costs you nothing.

Datasets and brief

The files are protected. Type the password from class; the files are unlocked in your browser.

The 12 questions (Part 1)

  1. Revenue vs quantity · are we truly expanding?
  2. Structural change · what changed between 2024 and 2025?
  3. Volume vs value · more units, or smarter selling?
  4. Strategic categories · which three deserve investment?
  5. Hidden strategic categories · weak in numbers, but essential?
  6. SKU pricing logic · popular, or simply expensive?
  7. Portfolio concentration · focused, or dangerously dependent?
  8. Discount performance · do discounts help our economics?
  9. Real growth from discounts? · extra revenue, or shifted volume?
  10. Campaign impact · extra sales, or demand that was there anyway?
  11. Channel concentration · where is the value, are we overexposed?
  12. Seasonality · are low months a weakness or by design?

Before you submit

  • Every answer slide: an insight headline with a number, one chart, the key number, a “so what” and a next step.
  • One axis per chart, bars from zero, units labelled.
  • Part 2 answers one Part 1 finding (name the question and the number).
  • Appendix: prompt log for every AI visual, AI-use disclosure slide, sources.
  • No likeness of real people, no copied campaigns or logos; real brand = “student concept”.
